# Create your account

You get one account, and it follows you into every group you join. The book club and the day job share a sign-in, so there's no fourth password to write on a sticky note.

Signing up is a few short questions. Each one has an answer filled in already, so **Continue** always works, and you can change any of it later.

## Sign up from an invite link

This is the quickest way in. Somebody in the group sent you a link, and the link already knows where you're going.

1. Open the invite link.
2. Select **Register using this invite**.
3. Check that **Invite code or link** holds your invite, then select **Continue**.
4. Fill in [About you](#fill-in-about-you) and select **Continue**.
5. [Choose how you'll sign in](#choose-how-youll-sign-in).

You land on the community's home page, already a member. Already have an account? Select **Sign in to accept** on the invite page, then **Accept invite**.

If the page says "Invite is no longer valid", the link ran out of time or uses. Ask whoever sent it for a fresh one; it takes them about four seconds.

## Sign up without an invite

Open your group's Initiative address and select **Register**, under "Need an account?" on the sign-in card. The first question is **What brings you here?**

| Pick | When | You end up with |
|---|---|---|
| **I have an invite** | Somebody gave you a code, or a link you'd rather paste | A place in their community |
| **Join a community** | You'd like to find a group, not run one | The community directory, open on what you're into |
| **Just for me** | You want somewhere for your own to-dos | A space nobody else can see, with a **To do** list ready |
| **For a group** | You're the one organizing the team, the committee, or the friends who have been "about to plan that trip" since March | A community with its first initiative, and an invite link to send around |

You only see the choices your server allows. On a server that takes new people by invite only, the page says **Invite required** and asks for your invite.

No **Register** link? Your server doesn't use passwords. Make your account with **Email me a code** if the sign-in card has it, or [with your work or school account](#sign-up-with-your-work-or-school-account).

### Start a space just for you

1. Select **Just for me**, then **Continue**.
2. Fill in [About you](#fill-in-about-you) and select **Continue**.
3. Change the **Name** and **Your first list** if you like, then select **Continue**.
4. [Choose how you'll sign in](#choose-how-youll-sign-in).

Initiative opens your **To do** list. Groceries go in first; the car registration you keep putting off can wait one more day.

### Start a community for your group

1. Select **For a group**, then **Continue**.
2. Fill in [About you](#fill-in-about-you) and select **Continue**.
3. Fill in **Community name**, **Description (optional)** and **First initiative**, then select **Continue**.
4. [Choose how you'll sign in](#choose-how-youll-sign-in).
5. On **Invite your people**, select **Copy** beside the **Invite link** and send it to your group.
6. Select **Go to** and your community's name.

An initiative is one area of work inside the community, like a team or an event. The link works for any number of people, so one message in the group chat covers everybody. You're the community's [superadmin](../guides/running-a-community.md#why-superadmin-is-separate), the top seat.

### Find a community to join

1. Select **Join a community**, then **Continue**.
2. Under **Where are you? (optional)**, say where you are if you'd like nearby communities first.
3. Under **Interests**, pick as many as you like, then select **Continue**. **Skip** shows you everything.
4. Fill in [About you](#fill-in-about-you), including your **Date of birth**, and select **Continue**.
5. [Choose how you'll sign in](#choose-how-youll-sign-in).

The directory opens on what you picked. Nothing far away is hidden, and nobody else sees where you said you are. Communities anyone can join are for people 16 and older, which is why this path asks your age.

### Paste an invite code

1. Select **I have an invite**, then **Continue**.
2. Paste the code or link into **Invite code or link** and select **Continue**.
3. Fill in [About you](#fill-in-about-you) and select **Continue**.
4. [Choose how you'll sign in](#choose-how-youll-sign-in).

## Fill in About you

- **Username** is the handle people find and mention you by. Initiative adds a number to the end, so you get the name you wanted even if three other Jordans got here first. Stuck? Tap one of the names under **Try one of these out!**
- **Timezone** is guessed from your browser and is usually right. It's the clock your due dates and reminders run on.
- **Date of birth** appears on servers that check ages. It's kept encrypted and used only to check age limits. You can leave it empty unless you're joining a community from the directory; you'll be asked once later.

## Choose how you'll sign in

The last step is **Create your account**. Type your **Email**, then pick one of the ways your server offers.

**With a password**

1. Type a **Password** of at least 12 characters, and type it again in **Confirm password**.
2. Select **Sign up**.

!!! tip "About that password"
    Four random words in a row is strong and surprisingly memorable. It beats the one with a capital letter at the front and an exclamation mark at the end that you've been quietly reusing since 2016. A password manager is better still.

**With a passkey**

A [passkey](../account/passkeys.md) lets your phone or computer sign you in with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.

1. Select **Create account with a passkey**.
2. Follow your browser's prompt.
3. Save the recovery codes Initiative shows you, with **Copy codes** or a pen.
4. Select **Done**.

Your account has no password, so those codes are how you'd set one if your passkey ever went missing. They're shown once.

**With a code by email**

1. Select **Email me a code instead**.
2. Check the address and select **Email me a code**.
3. Type the code from your inbox and select **Sign in**.
4. Check your username and select **Create account**.

The code works for 10 minutes. From then on you sign in the same way, with a fresh code each time.

Above the buttons there may be a spam check to tick, and a line listing your server's terms. Creating the account agrees to them.

## Confirm your email address

If your server sends email, you may see **Check your email** after signing up.

1. Open the email from Initiative and select its link.
2. Go back to the sign-in page in the same browser and sign in.

Initiative finishes setting up what you asked for, whether that's your space, your community or the directory. The link works for 24 hours. Nothing arrived after a few minutes? Check your spam folder, which is where these emails go to sulk.

## Sign up with your work or school account

Some servers let people in through an organization's own sign-in, called single sign-on. There's no form to fill in; your first sign-in makes the account.

1. On the sign-in page, select **Continue with** and your organization's name.
2. Sign in the way you do everywhere else at work or school.
3. On **Choose your username**, pick the name people will see and select **Continue**.
4. If **One thing before you start** appears, read your server's terms and select **I agree**.
5. If **How old are you?** appears, enter your **Date of birth** and select **Continue**.

Your organization may have put you in a community already, in which case you're in it. If not, Initiative asks **What brings you here?**, the same question as above without the account step at the end.

## Set up the first account on a new server

The very first person to open a brand-new server sees **Create the first account**. It asks for a **Username** and an **Email**, then a password or a passkey, and that account becomes the server's owner. If that's you, [Installation](../running-a-server/installation.md) covers what comes next.

??? techspec "How sign-up is gated"
    Whoever runs the server decides who can make an account, under **Platform Settings**:

    | Setting | What it does |
    |---|---|
    | **Let anyone sign up** | Off, only people with an invite can sign up. While community creation is off, only invited people can sign up either way. |
    | **Ways in** | Which of **Password**, **Passkey**, **Email OTP** and **Single sign-on** the sign-up and sign-in pages offer. |
    | **Check members' age** | Asks for a date of birth at sign-up and turns away anyone under the minimum age for an account where they are. |
    | Captcha | A spam check on the sign-up form and on asking for an emailed code. |

    An address is confirmed on the spot when the way in proved it, as an emailed code does, when the server has no outgoing email, and for the server's first account. Otherwise a confirmation link is sent, valid for 24 hours. An invite tied to a specific address waits until that address is confirmed.

    The first account on a new server becomes its platform **owner**. See [Platform roles](../running-a-server/platform-roles.md).

Next, [sign in](signing-in.md) whenever you come back.
