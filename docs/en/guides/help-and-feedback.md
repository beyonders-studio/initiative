# Getting help

Stuck? Initiative has two ways to get unstuck from inside the app: search these pages, or ask the people who run your server. There's also a way to tell the team that makes Initiative what you'd like it to do next.

## Search the help from inside the app

**Help** opens a panel that searches these pages without leaving what you're doing. You'll find it in your account menu (select your name at the bottom of the sidebar), in the command palette, and as the question mark in the desktop app's title bar.

1. Select **Help**.
2. Type what you're trying to do in **Search the user guide**.
3. Select an answer to read it.

Before you type anything, the panel shows answers **For this page**, picked for wherever you are in the app. **User guide** opens the whole of these pages in your browser.

## Ask the people who run your server

Where your server takes help requests, **Ask the team** sits under the search results. Your request goes to the people who run the server, not to your community's admins.

1. Select **Help**.
2. Search for your question first. What you type comes along into the request.
3. Select **Ask the team**.
4. In **What is it about?**, choose a topic.
5. Give it a **Title**, so it's easy to find again.
6. Say what happened in **What happened?**.
7. Add a picture if it helps: select the camera (**Picture this page**) to take one of the page you're on, select the paperclip (**Attach files**) to add a file, or paste or drop one on the box.
8. Select **Send**.

A picture of the page opens in an editor first. The people in it are already swapped out (names become Person 1, Person 2 and so on, you show as You, and profile pictures, emails and phone numbers are hidden). Select **Hide** to paint over anything else, or **Mark** to number the part that's wrong so your message can mention it.

The request also carries this device's details: the app's version, the platform, your language and theme, which page you were on (without anything that names what was on it) and the window's size. The line above **Send** says so, **See what** shows exactly what goes, and its switch leaves it all out.

What you can ask about depends on where you are:

| Topic | Offered |
|---|---|
| **My account**, **Billing**, **Something else** | Wherever the server takes help requests. |
| **This community** | Where the server takes help requests from the community you're in. |
| **A copy of this community's data, or deleting it** | To the community's superadmins, where the server takes these. |

Close the panel halfway through and your draft waits as a **Help request draft** button in the corner. Where the server takes no requests but has said who to write to, that address appears under the results instead, with a button to copy what you wrote.

Notices that tell you to get in touch, like the age question, a suspended community or a community out of seats, have **Ask for help** beside them. It opens the same request, already set to what the notice is about.

Whoever runs your server decides which communities can send help requests; see [Platform roles](../running-a-server/platform-roles.md).

## Follow a request in My Tickets

Everything you send lands in **My Tickets**, which appears in the sidebar of your [home page](your-space.md) once you've sent something.

1. Select **My Tickets**.
2. Select a request to read it.
3. Type in **Your reply** and select **Send reply** to answer the team.

Each one shows where it stands: **Received**, **In progress**, **Waiting on you** or **Closed**. You get a notification whenever the team replies or the request moves, so there's no need to keep checking. A closed request stays closed; if something else comes up, ask again.

## Send feedback to the team that makes Initiative

**Send feedback** is for an idea, something that got in your way, or something you love. It goes to the team that makes Initiative, not to whoever runs your server, so it's the place for "I wish it did this", and **Help** is the place for "I need this fixed today".

1. Open your account menu, or the command palette, and select **Send feedback**.
2. Choose **Suggest an idea**, **Something's not right** or **Give a compliment**.
3. Write it in the box. A screenshot pasted into the box comes along too.
4. Tick **You can contact me with questions about this** if the team may write back.
5. Select **Send feedback**.

Feedback lands on Initiative Cloud, the hosted service run by the team that makes Initiative. On any other server, **Send feedback** opens Cloud's feedback page in your browser, and sending takes an Initiative Cloud account; your own server sends nothing along the way. **See what** shows exactly what else goes with it (the app's version, platform, language, theme, which page you were on and the window's size), and its switch leaves that out.

Without the tick, feedback goes one way. With it, the team can write to you about it, and their messages show up in **My Tickets** on Initiative Cloud.

## Vote for ideas

The ideas the team hears most often end up on the **Ideas** page on Initiative Cloud, written up by the team, and anybody can read it, signed in or not. **See what others have asked for**, under the feedback box, opens it.

Your idea may already be there. Once you've written a few words, **Ideas like yours** appear under the box. Pick one and send, and what you wrote reaches the team attached to that idea, next to the eleven other people who asked for the same thing. Select **Vote** on any idea you want, and you'll hear each time one of yours moves along.
