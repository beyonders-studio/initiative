# Search & shortcuts

The fastest way around Initiative is typing. Search finds anything in the community you're in, and the same box jumps to your own pages, starts a task, and opens Help.

## Find something

Search looks through the community you're in, and its name is in the search box so you always know which one. Open search by pressing ++cmd+k++ on a Mac or ++ctrl+k++ on Windows or Linux, selecting the search box at the top of a community's sidebar, selecting the magnifying glass in the bar along the bottom of a phone, or tapping the screen with three fingers.

1. Open search.
2. Type what you're looking for.
3. Use the arrow keys to pick a result.
4. Press ++enter++ to open it.

Results appear as you type, best match first. The last word can be half finished, so `bud` finds the budget. While you're typing, ++tab++ moves between **Tools**, **Members**, **Comments** and **Tags**, and ++"Esc"++ closes the box.

![Searching with the Command Center](../images/search/command-center.png)

### What search looks through

| | What it finds |
|---|---|
| **Tools** | Projects, files and the collections they're in, calendars, counter groups, dashboards, posts, wikis and contacts, by name. |
| **What's inside them** | Tasks, calendar events, counters, wiki pages and pictures. |
| **What's written in files** | The words in a text document, the text on a whiteboard, and the cells and sheet names in a spreadsheet. |
| **Comments** | On any of the above. |
| **Tags and people** | Search for somebody's name or handle, in whole words, and you also find what mentions them. |

It only ever shows what you can already open. Something you can't see doesn't turn up, and doesn't hint that it exists either.

### When you can't spell it

Most of us can't, most of the time, and that's fine. If nothing matches what you typed, search shows the closest names it can find, and says above them that that's what they are. People's names work the same way, so you don't have to ask your treasurer how to spell her name a third time.

Every picker in Initiative uses the same search, from mentioning something with `#` or linking with `[[` to attaching a file or choosing what a smart chip points at. See [Mentions & links](mentions-and-links.md).

## See every result

The search box shows the first few matches. For the full list:

1. Open search and type what you're looking for.
2. Select **See all results for** what you typed, at the bottom of the results.

The results page opens on **Tools**, with **Members**, **Comments** and **Tags** beside it. A tab with nothing behind it is grayed out rather than letting you click into an empty page. Each result shows the line that matched, with your words highlighted, so you can tell which of five files called "Budget" is the right one before opening any of them.

Archived things are left out until you turn on **Include archived**. The address holds your search, tab and page, so a page of results is a link you can send somebody.

### What comes first

The best match usually comes first. When two things match about as well, the one more of your community has been opening or working on lately goes a little higher, so the meeting notes four people have lived in all week beat the ones somebody made in March and never opened again.

Something needs three different people before it counts at all, and opening a page forty times counts as opening it once. It only reorders what you'd have found anyway, and nobody can see who opened what.

To keep your own activity out of it:

1. Open **My Settings → Privacy**.
2. Under **Search ranking**, turn off **Count my activity toward search ranking**.

It stops counting from the next hourly update. A community can turn ranking off for everyone, too; see [Ranking search by engagement](../security/community-security.md#ranking-search-by-engagement).

## Go somewhere, or start something, from search

Open search without typing and it's a menu:

| Group | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Actions** | **Add Task** and **Add file** where you can create them, **Help**, and **Send feedback** and **Report a security problem** where your server offers them. |
| **Suggested** | What you opened most recently. |
| **Pages** | **Home**, **My Calendar**, **My Tools**, **My Messages**, **My Stats**, **My Settings** and **All Initiatives**, **My Tickets** once you've sent a help request, and **Community Settings** and the server's own settings pages if you can open them. |
| **Tasks** | Your open tasks, most recently changed first. |

Start typing and the menu narrows down to whatever matches, along with the search results.

## Look something up in the user guide

**Help** searches this user guide from inside the app, and shows answers for the page you're on before you type anything. It's in your account menu at the bottom of the sidebar, in search, and behind the question mark in the desktop app's title bar. [Getting help](help-and-feedback.md) walks through it, and through asking the people who run your server when the guide doesn't have the answer.

## Go back to something you had open

The things you open (projects, files, counter groups and the like) line up as tabs along the top of the page, like tabs in a browser.

- Select a tab to go back to it.
- Select a tab's **X** to close it.
- Right-click a tab for **Close others** or **Close all**.

To change how many tabs it keeps:

1. Open **My Settings → Preferences**.
2. Set **Recent items in tab bar** to any number from 1 to 100.

## Keys worth knowing

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| ++cmd+k++ / ++ctrl+k++ | Open or close search |
| ++tab++ | In search, move between Tools, Members, Comments and Tags |
| ++"Esc"++ | Close search or a dialog |
| ++shift++ + click a column heading | Sort a table by one more column |

The rest are on [Keyboard shortcuts](../reference/keyboard-shortcuts.md).

!!! tip "When in doubt, search"
    Can't remember which initiative a file ended up in? Going looking for it is how you lose twenty minutes and your good mood. ++cmd+k++ and a few letters is faster than the sidebar nearly every time, and after a week or so your fingers do it before you've decided to.
