# Your space

Communities and initiatives keep everybody's work in its place, which is great right up until you're in four of them and just want to know what you're supposed to do today. Your space is the part of Initiative that's only about you: a home page you arrange yourself, and a few pages that gather your things from every community at once.

Signing in puts you here. To come back from anywhere, select the Initiative logo at the top of the community rail, or the house in the bar along the bottom of a phone. The sidebar switches to your own pages:

| Page | What's on it |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your page of widgets, laid out however you like. |
| **My Calendar** | Your events and dated tasks, from every community. |
| **My Tools** | Everything that's been shared with you, from every community. |
| **My Messages** | Private conversations. See [Messages](messages.md). |
| **My Tickets** | Help requests you've sent. It appears once you've sent one. |
| **My Stats** | Your streak, and how the week went. |

Nothing you do here changes anything for anybody else. Rearrange Home six times before lunch if you like; nobody gets a notification about it.

## Arrange your home page { #home }

Home is a page of widgets, and it starts out arranged for whatever brought you here when you signed up: running your own to-do list, starting a community, or joining one. A **Getting started** card sits near the top with a few first things to do, and ticks each one off once you've actually done it. Select **Hide getting started** on it whenever you've had enough.

### Move, resize or remove a widget

1. On **Home**, select **Customize**.
2. Drag a widget to move it.
3. Pull a widget's corner to make it bigger or smaller.
4. To take a widget off the page, select the **X** on it.
5. Select **Done**.

On a phone the widgets stack in one column, so step 2 is the up and down arrows on each widget, and there's no resizing to do.

### Add a widget

1. Select **Customize**.
2. Select **Add widget**.
3. Pick a widget. It goes at the bottom of the page.
4. Drag it where you want it.
5. Select **Done**.

A few widgets only make sense once per page (two greetings would be a lot), and the picker marks those **Already on your page**. The rest can go on as many times as you like, so you can have an **Activity** widget for each community, or one **Clock and timer** showing the time and another counting down.

| Widget | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Greeting** | Hello, today's date, and how much is overdue or due today, with Yonder reacting to it. |
| **Getting started** | A few first things to do, ticked off as you do them. |
| **Focus** | What needs doing now. See [below](#see-what-needs-doing-now). |
| **My tasks** | Everything assigned to you, from every community, as a table. |
| **Coming up** | Events and task dates over the next few days. Its title opens My Calendar. |
| **My calendar** | The same calendar as [My Calendar](#my-calendar), by month or week. |
| **Recent** | What you opened lately, or what changed lately, everywhere. The two buttons on the widget switch between them. |
| **Bookmarks** | Everything you've [bookmarked](#bookmarks), from every community. |
| **Activity** | What people changed lately in one community. |
| **My communities** | Every community you're in, as its card. |
| **My progress** | Your streak, your week, and how much lands on time. |
| **Clock and timer** | The time here and in up to three other places, or a countdown. |

### Change what a widget shows

Most widgets have a settings button. The ones that don't have nothing to set.

1. Select **Customize**.
2. Select the settings button on the widget.
3. Change what you like. It saves as you go.
4. Select **Done** in the settings.
5. Select **Done** on the page.

| Widget | What you can set |
|---|---|
| **Greeting** | **Show Yonder**, for days you'd rather the mascot didn't comment. |
| **My tasks** | **Add new tasks to**: a list the widget adds tasks to. See [Add a task from Home](#add-a-task-from-home). |
| **Coming up** | **How far ahead**: the next 3, 7, 14 or 30 days. |
| **Recent** and **Bookmarks** | **Tools**: only these kinds of tool. Leave it empty for all of them. |
| **Activity** | **Community**, and **Show**: **All** changes, or only those to **Yours**. |
| **Clock and timer** | **Other time zones** (up to three), and **Timer length, in minutes**. |

Switch the clock to a timer with the buttons on the widget itself. Select the time to type a length, like `25` or `5:30`, or pick one of the lengths underneath. A running timer keeps going when you close the tab, and shows the same countdown on your phone, so the pizza gets out of the oven either way. The speaker button mutes it.

### Start a page over from a layout

Take every widget off a page and it offers **Start from a layout**, which fills it with one of the ready-made layouts listed under [Make a new view](#make-a-new-view). **Add widget** is beside it if you'd rather build from scratch.

## Keep more than one home page

Here for more than one reason? Keep a view for each, and switch between them with tabs at the top of Home: work on one, the soccer league on another. You can have up to 10.

### Make a new view

1. On **Home**, select **Customize**.
2. Select **+** beside the tabs.
3. Pick **Blank**, or one of the layouts below.
4. Select **Done**.

| Layout | Made for |
|---|---|
| **My tasks** | Your list, with what needs doing now on top. The plainest of the lot. |
| **My day** | Running your own life: what's urgent, the days ahead, your streak, then the whole list. |
| **Heads down** | Getting through it: a timer and the few things that need doing now. |
| **Assigned work** | Work that's handed to you: your list, the urgent part beside it, and what's happened to your things. |
| **Community** | Following a club: your communities, what's happening in them, and what's coming up. |
| **Running the show** | Keeping a community moving: its activity, your bookmarks, what changed in projects and dashboards, and your list. |

More layouts are in your own marketplace: **Browse the marketplace** sits at the bottom of the same picker. Choosing **Add to my home** on one opens it as a new view, and the views you already have stay as they are. See [Home layouts](plugins-and-marketplace.md#home-layouts).

Home opens on whichever view you used last, and the address names it, so a view can be a browser bookmark.

### Rename, copy, reorder or delete a view

1. Select **Customize**.
2. Open the view's tab.
3. Select **⋯** beside the tabs.
4. Choose **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Move left**, **Move right** or **Delete view**.
5. Select **Done**.

Deleting a view takes its widgets with it and nothing else. Your communities never hear about it.

### Put a banner on a view

Each view can wear a banner of its own, so the drama club's view gets the stage and the work view gets nothing at all.

1. Open the view, then select **Customize**.
2. Select **Banner**.
3. Pick a banner, **Use my profile banner**, or **No banner**.
4. Select **Done**.

The choices are the banners you have: the ones that come with Initiative and the ones from [decoration packs](plugins-and-marketplace.md#decoration-packs) you've picked up. **Get more banners** opens the marketplace. Give a pack back and its banner comes off the view too.

## See what needs doing now

**Focus** is the short list: overdue work, plus whatever's due soon enough for its priority. Out of the box, urgent and high-priority tasks show whatever their date, and medium and low ones show two days out. Tick something off and it stays until tomorrow, struck through, while the count at the top goes up. Watching the pile shrink is the whole point.

### Pin a task to Focus

Pin anything you want kept in view whatever its date: the thing you promised on the phone, or the thing that isn't due for weeks but will ruin the week it is.

1. Point at the task in **Focus** or in the **My tasks** table.
2. Select its pin (**Pin to Focus**).

To unpin it, select the pin again (**Remove from Focus**).

### Change how far ahead Focus looks

1. Select **Focus settings** on the Focus widget.
2. Drag the slider for each priority, from **Today** up to 30 days, or all the way to **Any date**.

Overdue work always shows, and pinned tasks show whatever these say.

## Work through your tasks

The **My tasks** widget is every task assigned to you, from every community. **Group by** sorts it under dates or under communities, **Filters** narrows it down, and the box above the table finds a task by name. Dated tasks show up on [My Calendar](#my-calendar) too.

### Add a task from Home

Give the widget a list and it gets a line for adding tasks straight to it, already assigned to you.

1. Select **Customize**.
2. Select the settings button on **My tasks**.
3. Under **Add new tasks to**, pick a list.
4. Select **Done** in the settings.
5. Select **Done** on the page.
6. Type the task on the new line at the top of the widget.
7. Select **Add**.

## Find everything shared with you { #my-tools }

**My Tools** is every [tool](tools.md) that's reached you, from every community, in one table. Each row says which community and initiative it lives in, so you can stop playing "which one was that in again".

1. Select **My Tools** in the sidebar.
2. Pick a kind of tool along the top.
3. To see only what you made, select **Made by me**. **Everything** goes back.
4. In more than one community? Pick some under **Communities** to see only theirs.

A kind of tool you have none of anywhere doesn't get a tab. Never touched a counter? No counters tab, and no empty table sulking behind it.

"Reached you" means shared with you, shared with a role you hold, or shared with everyone in an initiative you're in. It doesn't mean everything you could open if you went looking. A community admin, who can open anything in their community, gets their own work here rather than a 400-row list of everybody else's.

The tool, search, sort, page and communities are all in the address, so whatever you're looking at is a link you can send somebody. That beats "open My Tools and click around a bit".

## See all your dates in one calendar { #my-calendar }

**My Calendar** puts your events and your dated tasks from every community on one calendar, by month or by week. If three groups each believe their thing is the only thing happening in your week, this is where that gets settled.

Finished tasks are hidden to start with. The controls above the calendar choose which calendars and projects show, and filter by status category, priority or community. The **My calendar** widget on Home is the same calendar, filters and all.

## Check your streak { #my-stats }

**My Stats** is a friendly look at your own work. Pick a community at the top, or leave it on **All Communities**.

| | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Current Streak** | Days in a row you finished something. |
| **On-Time Rate** | How often you finish before the due date. |
| **Avg Completion** | How long a task takes you, from start to done. |
| **Backlog Trend** | Whether your pile grew or shrank this week. |
| **Tasks Completed** | This week, and all time. |
| **Velocity** | Tasks assigned to you against tasks you finished, each week for 12 weeks. |
| **Tasks by Community** | Where your finished tasks came from. |
| **Activity Heatmap** | Your past year, a square a day. |

Only you can see it. There's no leaderboard, and no manager is getting a weekly summary. It exists because watching a streak grow is nice, and that turned out to be reason enough.

## Follow a help request you sent

Every help request you send lands in **My Tickets**, along with any report you've filed, and the page shows up in your sidebar once there's something on it. Each one says where it stands (**Received**, **In progress**, **Waiting on you** or **Closed**), and you get a notification when it moves, so there's no need to keep checking. [Getting help](help-and-feedback.md#follow-a-request-in-my-tickets) walks through answering the team, and through sending a request in the first place.

## Bookmark something { #bookmarks }

Any tool can be bookmarked: a project, a file, a wiki, the counter of how many raffle tickets are left.

1. Open it, or find it in its list.
2. Select **Bookmark** beside its name, or on its card or row.

Bookmarks show in **Bookmarks** at the top of that community's sidebar, and in the **Bookmarks** widget on Home, from every community. Select **Remove bookmark** to let one go. Bookmarks are yours alone, so bookmarking the committee minutes doesn't pin them to anybody else's screen, and leaving a community takes your bookmarks there with you.

## Subscribe to something { #subscribing }

A bookmark keeps a thing close. A subscription keeps you told: when somebody changes it, comments on it or reacts to it, you hear about it in your [notifications](notifications.md), and it rises in the activity on the community's front page. Subscribe to the bake sale project and every new task, schedule swap and increasingly tense comment about the folding tables reaches you.

You're subscribed to anything you made from the moment you make it. Things you're assigned to or invited to reach you under **Assigned to you** without subscribing at all.

1. Open the thing.
2. Select **Subscribe**, the bell near its name.

| To subscribe to | The bell is |
|---|---|
| A tool | Beside the bookmark, next to its name. It covers everything inside it. |
| A task or an event | With the other buttons at the top of it. |
| A picture or a counter | Beside its name. For a picture, that's under **Details**. |
| A wiki | Beside its name at the top of the wiki's sidebar. Each page has its own, beside the wiki's bookmark at the top of the page. |

Select **Unsubscribe** to stop, even on something you made. Your own changes never notify you, and a flurry of edits to one thing arrives as one line rather than twelve. If it's still too much, turn **Subscribed** down under [My Settings → Notifications](notifications.md#choose-what-you-hear-about). What you subscribe to is yours alone, and leaving a community takes your subscriptions there with you.
