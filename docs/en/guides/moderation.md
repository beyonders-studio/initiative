# Reporting and moderation

Most groups never need this page. But on the day somebody posts something that shouldn't be there, it helps to know that anyone can flag it, and that the right people can deal with it quietly.

## Report something

Anyone signed in can report comments, notices, pictures, wiki pages, events, profiles and community directory listings. Your own things don't offer it, because you can already edit or delete those.

1. Select the small flag, **Report**, on the thing.
2. In **What is wrong?**, choose a reason.
3. If you chose **Illegal**, choose **Which law?**.
4. Add a line in **Anything else?** if you like. For **Illegal** and **Other** a line is required, under **Tell them what you saw**.
5. Select **Send report**.

As the report opens, it takes a picture of what you're reporting and attaches it, with the people in it left showing, since a report is usually about someone. Select the picture to hide parts of it or mark them, or remove it before you send.

| What you report | Who looks at it |
|---|---|
| Anything inside an initiative, like a comment, a notice or a picture | That initiative's Moderators, and the community's admins |
| A profile, or a community's directory listing | Whoever runs the server |

The people who look see what you wrote. Nothing happens to the thing you reported until one of them decides it should, and the person who made it is never told who reported it.

A report of something **illegal** goes straight to whoever runs the server as well as to the community's moderators. A child-safety report takes no attachments: say where it is, and it stays exactly where it is for them to deal with.

A report that reaches whoever runs the server is listed in your **My Tickets**, though reports don't get replies there. Where the server takes no reports, the thank-you tells you who to contact instead.

## Moderate an initiative

An initiative's **Moderators**, and the community's admins, moderate it. They see one extra entry at the top of the initiative in the sidebar: **Moderation**. Nobody else sees it, and nothing switches it off. Project Managers run the work rather than moderate it, so they can delete their own comments but not other people's; to let somebody moderate, a community admin puts them on the Moderator role (see [Initiative roles](../sharing/initiative-roles.md)).

The **Moderation** page has four tabs:

| Tab | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Reports** | What people have reported here, **Open** and **Settled**. Each shows what was reported, how many people reported it, and what they said. |
| **Members** | Who's in the initiative, and what each of them may do. |
| **Sharing** | How widely each thing here is shared. Open one to change it. |
| **Log** | What moderators have done here, newest first: what was removed and why, what a removed comment said, locks and warnings. |

### Settle a report

1. Select **Moderation** at the top of the initiative in the sidebar.
2. On **Reports**, open the report.
3. Open the reported thing to look at it where it lives, then come back.
4. Add a note in the box if you like, saying what you found and why you decided.
5. Choose **Dismiss**, **Remove…**, **Warn…** or **Send to the platform**.

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Dismiss** | Nothing needs doing. The report is settled. |
| **Remove…** | Takes it down. Choose **Why** (the reason it was reported for, unless you pick another), add a **Note for the log** if you like, and select **Take it down**. A comment becomes a line saying it was removed and why, and its replies stay; anything else goes to the trash. Whoever made it is told why, never by whom. |
| **Warn…** | Sends whoever made it a notice from the moderators, in your words, under **What they're told**. Select **Send warning**. It never says who reported it. |
| **Send to the platform** | Hands the report to whoever runs the server. See [Send a report to the platform](#send-a-report-to-the-platform). |

A report of something illegal shows **Platform notified**: it went to whoever runs the server as well, and they look at it whatever you decide here.

### Act without a report

You don't need a report to act. Moderators and community admins get a shield menu, **Moderate**, on comments, posts, pictures, wiki pages, events and tasks:

- **Remove…** takes it down, as above.
- **Warn…** sends whoever made it a notice, as above, and leaves the thing up.
- **Clear reactions** takes every reaction off a comment or post. It can't be undone.
- **Hold for the platform…** hides it and keeps it exactly as it is (see below). You won't see it either once it's held.

### Lock a comment thread

Select **Lock comments** at the top of any comment thread, on a task, a wiki page or anything else. The thread still reads, but nobody adds to it or edits what's in it, moderators included, and authors can still delete their own comments. The thing's owner can't unlock it; a moderator can, with **Unlock comments**.

### Put something back

1. Select **Moderation**, then **Log**.
2. Find the removal.
3. Select **Put back**.

It comes back exactly as it was.

### Send a report to the platform

Some reports aren't the community's to settle. **Send to the platform** hands one to whoever runs the server, with what the reporters wrote and attached, and asks one question: while they look, should the thing stay up?

1. On the report, select **Send to the platform**.
2. Under **While they look at it**, choose **Leave it up while they look** or **Hide it now, and keep it exactly as it is**.
3. If you're hiding it, choose **Why** (a legal request to keep it, or that it may be illegal) and, if it may be illegal, **Which law**.
4. Add a **Note for the platform** if you like, such as who asked and a reference number. Only the platform can read it.
5. Select **Send**, or **Hide and send**.

Use the hiding option when the police or a court asked you to keep something, or it may be illegal. A report of something illegal starts with it already chosen. The report closes here, and whoever runs the server decides what happens next.

Hidden content is **held for the platform**:

- Nobody in the community can see it, community admins and you included. Anything inside it is held too, like a project's tasks or a wiki's pages, along with the pictures and files it shows.
- Nobody in the community can change it or delete it. Deleting what it sits in leaves it in place, and emptying the trash can't remove it.
- Replies to a held comment stay, under an **Unavailable** placeholder.
- Only whoever runs the server can release it: putting it back as it was, moving it to the trash, or deleting it for good.
- Nobody is told. To its author, it just isn't there.
