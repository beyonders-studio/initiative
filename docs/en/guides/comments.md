# Comments & reactions

Everything in Initiative has a comment thread under it: every project, task, file, event, post, wiki page, contact and the rest. The conversation about a thing sits on that thing, so nobody has to remember which of the four group chats the decision was made in, or scroll back to March to find it.

Anyone who can see something can comment on it.

## Comment on something

1. Open the thing.
2. Scroll to **Comments**. On a wiki page, select the **Comments** button above the page.
3. Type in the box.
4. Select **Post Comment**, or press ++ctrl+enter++ (++cmd+enter++ on a Mac).

Your comment shows straight away for everyone who can see the thing. People who follow it hear about it (see [who hears about a comment](#who-hears-about-a-comment)).

## Format a comment

Comments take Markdown, and the formatting bar above the box writes it for you: **Heading**, **Bold**, **Italic**, **Strikethrough**, **Link**, **Quote**, **Code**, **Bulleted list**, **Numbered list** and **Task list**. On a narrow screen some of these are under **More formatting**.

**Preview** shows how it'll look, and **Write** goes back to typing.

## Mention someone or link to something in a comment

- Type `@` and a name to mention a person, or select **Mention a member**. They get a notification.
- Type `#` and a name to link anything in the initiative, or select **Link anything in this initiative**.
- Type `[[` to link to a tool, or make a new one from the name you typed.

[Mentions & links](mentions-and-links.md) has the details.

## Add a picture to a comment

A screenshot says it faster than three paragraphs.

- Paste a picture into the box, or drag one onto it.
- Or select **Image** and choose **Upload a picture**, or **From Files** for one somebody already uploaded. On the phone app, **Image** offers **Take a photo** and **Choose from library**.

Take the picture back out of your comment before posting and it's gone from storage too.

## Reply to a comment

1. Select **Reply** under the comment.
2. Type your reply.
3. Select **Reply**.

The person you replied to hears about it.

## Edit or delete your comment

To edit:

1. Select **Edit** under your comment.
2. Change the words.
3. Select **Save**.

An edited comment says **(edited)** next to its time.

To delete:

1. Select **Delete** (the bin) under your comment.
2. Select **Delete** to confirm.

It goes to your trash, where you can restore it. If people replied to it, their replies stay, under a line saying you deleted yours.

## React to a comment

A reaction answers without writing a whole reply: agreement, thanks, or the universally understood "I have read this and have nothing to add."

- Select **React with 👍** for a quick thumbs up.
- Select **Add a reaction** to pick any emoji. Common ones come first, and everything is searchable.
- Select a reaction somebody else added to add yours to it, and select it again to take yours back.
- Hover a reaction to see who added it.

Anyone who can see the comment can react. Its author gets one line in the bell right away, and email or push (if they've left those on) waits a little and bundles a flurry of thumbs into one message. Posts take reactions too; see [Posts](posts.md).

## Turn comments off for one thing

Some things don't need a discussion, like the room booking. Whoever can edit a tool can switch its thread off.

1. Open the tool (a project, file, calendar and so on).
2. Select **Settings**.
3. On the **Details** tab, turn off **Comments**.

The thread comes off its page. Nothing is deleted, and it all comes back when you switch it on again. A task keeps its own comments whatever its project says.

## Lock a thread

Moderators can stop a heated thread without deleting anything. **Lock comments**, at the top of the thread, freezes it, and everyone sees "A moderator locked comments here." **Unlock comments** opens it again. See [Working with initiatives](initiatives.md) for who moderates.

## Who hears about a comment

| Who | Hears about | Switch in [Notifications](notifications.md) |
|---|---|---|
| Somebody you mentioned with `@` | Your comment | **Mentions and replies** |
| The person you replied to | Your reply | **Mentions and replies** |
| A comment's author | Reactions to it | **Mentions and replies** |
| Everyone following the thing, which includes whoever made it | New comments | **Subscribed** |
| Whoever a task is assigned to | New comments on the task | **Subscribed** |
| Whoever a task is assigned to | A comment anywhere that links the task with `#` | **Mentions and replies** |

A long thread shows its newest comments first; **Load older comments** at the end fetches more.
