## Still stuck?

- For quick answers, try the [FAQ](../faq.md).
- To find out how to do something, look in [Using Initiative](../guides/index.md).
- For trouble getting into your account, see [Getting started](../getting-started/index.md).

- For a security concern, see [Reporting a problem](../security/reporting-a-problem.md).
- Inside the app, **Help** in your account menu searches these pages without leaving what you're doing.
